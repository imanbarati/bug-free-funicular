# Handoff Summary: Job Survival Strategies in Iran (Climate AI Background → General Graduate Employment)

## Context

The user is a **college graduate in Iran** (likely with climate science / Earth science / AI-related background) seeking **practical, trainable employment** in a constrained, sanctions-affected, third-world economy. They explicitly clarified they are **not** trying to start an AI business — they want **any job that a university graduate can be trained into**.

## What Was Covered

### Turn 1 — Climate AI job survival in Iran
Original question framed around surviving in "climate AI" work in backward Iran. The response laid out structural constraints (chip sanctions, cloud service bans, unstable internet/power, data bandwidth limits) and proposed strategies: remote arbitrage, downgrading to data annotation/RLHF, lightweight/edge AI, offline-first workflows. **The user rejected this framing** — they don't want AI business advice.

### Turn 2 — English restatement
Same content restated in English. Still AI-business-focused. User rejected again.

### Turn 3 — Pivot to general graduate employment (the real ask)
User clarified: **"any business for college graduate trainable"** — i.e., any trainable job for a university graduate, not AI entrepreneurship.

Response covered:
- **Harsh market reality**: 35%+ of unemployed hold degrees; 57% of graduates cite "no good job market" as reason for dissatisfaction; labor participation rate ~40%; 40M+ "economically inactive."
- **Direction 1 — Private-sector talent programs**:
  - National Elites Foundation "Third Call" — for bachelor's/master's/PhD grads, private companies, register at **sina.bmn.ir**
  - **TalentCo** — master's/PhD under 35, register at **pishro.aif.ir**
  - Fakoor Sanat "Fakoor Gen" — **only** six top universities (Sharif, Tehran, Amirkabir, etc.), 30 of 1,594 applicants — skip if not eligible
- **Direction 2 — Entry-level, no experience required**: International Researchers Institute (آگاه) in Tehran hiring statisticians/interviewers — diploma+, no experience, no major restriction, social security day one, free training, flexible hours.
- **Direction 3 — Solar panel installation/maintenance**: explicitly targeting educated job-seeking youth; trainable; real demand as Iran builds solar plants.
- **Mindset shift**: National Skills University graduates have 64%+ employment because 70%+ of coursework is practical. Employers want people who can work, not degrees. Emphasize willingness to be trained over major.
- **Action sequence**: register Sina + TalentCo (free) → find entry-level cash-flow job in Tehran → explore solar training. Definition of "right job" = someone willing to pay and train you.

- Internet instability, VPN-driven IP bans on Upwork/AWS-type platforms
- Some global AI task platforms (e.g., Mercor) explicitly exclude Iran
- Degrees heavily devalued; participation rate low
- Sanctions block high-end compute and many international cloud/services
